Track record

Every call gets saved here, then checked later against what gold actually did. After a few weeks this tells you whether any of it is worth listening to.
Candles come from PAXG on Binance — a token backed by one ounce of gold in a vault — and get multiplied onto the real XAU/USD spot scale using the live PAXG-to-gold ratio from xaus.com. So the prices you see are real gold prices. The ratio is fixed at the moment the chart loads, so older candles carry today's ratio rather than the one that applied back then; the premium moves slowly so that's a few cents, not dollars. Switch to PAXG on the toggle to see the raw token instead.
Gaps, equal highs and lows, structure and candle patterns are all worked out in the code from the actual candles, not guessed by the AI.
